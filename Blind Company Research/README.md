## 🚀 Set up

1. Follow [onboarding](ONBOARDING.md) to create or reuse the shared Application Workflow installation and its Excel or Google Sheets tracker.
2. Review the [103-company starter list](blind-companies.json), or supply your own. Choose companies and job criteria; Codex saves your working copy privately.
3. Verify the research and central-save path, then choose manual runs or your own schedule.

**The starter has no personal notes. Ratings are historical and not freshly verified; original rating dates are unknown.** Career links are rechecked during research. You can change the rating threshold and targets.

```text
Read Blind Company Research/ONBOARDING.md and guide me through setup.
Reuse my existing private installation if I have one. Enable only the sources
I choose, verify research and the shared tracker handoff, and save my setup summary.
```

You may choose this source on its own: **LinkedIn alerts, Gmail, referrals, Calendar and Messages are optional.** Local Excel needs no Google connection. See [shared tool setup](../ONBOARDING.md#tools-for-your-choices).

**Computer use:** only where career pages need browser interaction. The supplied ratings need no Blind login.

## ▶️ Run

Ask your configured Blind chat to [run company research](run.md).

1. Check every enabled company against your saved criteria.
2. Queue verified roles and honest coverage gaps for the central chat.
3. Run the central chat to save them to the **same jobs tracker** used by LinkedIn alerts.

A queued result is not saved until the central writer verifies it. If central runs manually, its next run imports the results; a Blind schedule does not create a central schedule.

Then request [Application Batches](<../Application Batches/README.md>) to open eligible pages. You fill and submit applications.
