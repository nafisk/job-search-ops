# Job Search Ops

Choose a workflow and give your agent its runbook.

| Workflow | What it does | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Resume](Resume/README.md) | Review and rewrite engineering experience bullets | Your resume |
| [LinkedIn profile](LinkedIn/README.md) | Audit your profile and propose edits | Your resume and profile content, with authorized browser access for live work |
| [Application Discovery](<Application Discovery/README.md>) | Find jobs through LinkedIn alerts, referral portals or Blind company research and save them to one tracker | Local files, Python, a tracker and access to your selected sources |
| [Application Batches](<Application Batches/README.md>) | Open eligible application pages for you to complete | A completed shared setup and pilot, saved jobs, confirmed criteria and browser access |
| [Recruiter Outreach](<Recruiter Outreach/README.md>) | Find recruiters and prepare LinkedIn drafts for you to send | An authorized browser and a separate contact tracker |

## Get started

For a resume or LinkedIn content review, supply the material and the complete runbook. For the other workflows, keep the complete repo or release ZIP contents in a permanent folder and follow [onboarding](ONBOARDING.md). Keep settings and personal data in a separate private folder.

Alerts, referrals and Blind research can use the same tracker. The central run verifies and saves postings before Batches can open them. Request Batches and Outreach separately. For scheduled runs, see the verification requirements in the [Application Discovery guide index](<Application Discovery/docs/source-map.md>).
