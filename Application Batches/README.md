## 🚀 Set up

1. Complete [Application Workflow onboarding](<../Application Workflow/ONBOARDING.md>) first.
2. Follow [batch onboarding](ONBOARDING.md) to select your browser and private ledger.
3. Choose your batch limits. Defaults: **five pages first, five more next, twenty outstanding maximum**.

Codex needs your existing tracker, saved role restrictions, Python 3.10+ and supported browser control. It uses your selected Excel or Google Sheets access. No additional app, extension or schedule is required.

## ▶️ Open a batch

Ask your batch chat: **“Read the batch runbook and my private ledger. Open my first batch of eligible application pages.”**

1. Recover earlier tabs and check available capacity.
2. Check exact jobs, current availability, eligibility and referral holds.
3. Open accessible employer/ATS application pages and return a short review list.

You fill and submit. Codex leaves your other tabs and edits intact. A closed tab never proves that you applied.

## ⏭️ Continue when ready

**“Next five”** requests five additional pages within your remaining capacity. **“Check all submissions”** separately checks registered applications using your selected evidence sources. Neither action starts recruiter outreach.
