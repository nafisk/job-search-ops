# Resume

Review software engineering experience one role or a few bullets at a time.

## How to use

1. Share your current resume and the complete [resume runbook](resume-content-optimization.md). Add a target role, level or posting if you have one.
2. Ask the assistant to review a role or a few bullets. Confirm the facts about your work, ownership and results.
3. Review the proposed wording. If you want a file edit, authorize the assistant to apply the approved wording to a new copy.

For a full candidate draft, provide verified supporting material and explicitly request **EVIDENCE-PACK DRAFT** mode. The runbook defines the draft and decision log.

## Change your targets

Share the revised role, level or posting. Ask the assistant to reuse confirmed facts and flag wording that needs another review.
