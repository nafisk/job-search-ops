## 🚀 How to use

**Required:** your current resume and an assistant that can read the complete runbook. No Codex, Python or account setup is required for content review. [Repository onboarding](../ONBOARDING.md) covers the other workflows.

1. Give your AI assistant the **entire [runbook](resume-content-optimization.md)**: attach the Markdown file, paste its full contents, or point a file-enabled agent to its path.
2. Share your current resume. Add your target engineering role, level, and a job description if you have one.
3. Ask the agent to read the whole file and use it as the guiding instructions for improving your resume.
4. Answer its short questions, review suggested wording, and approve the bullets you want to use.

> [!TIP]
> Your current resume is enough to begin. The agent can help identify which bullets to improve first.

After supplying the resume and runbook, ask:

```text
Read the complete Resume/resume-content-optimization.md runbook.
Review my resume one role or a few bullets at a time. Ask for missing facts,
and let me approve the wording before editing a file.
```

## What to expect

- Focused questions about what you built, owned, and achieved.
- A recommended rewrite, with alternatives when there is a meaningful wording tradeoff.
- Clear separation between confirmed facts and unresolved questions.
- Approved wording applied to a new copy only when you authorize editing.

For a complete candidate draft, supply verified supporting material and explicitly request **EVIDENCE-PACK DRAFT** mode. The runbook guides the agent through an evidence-backed rewrite and decision log for your review.

## Change your targets

Tell the assistant your revised role, level or job description. Reuse confirmed facts and review any new wording; changing a target does not change your experience. The conversation should end with approved wording, unresolved questions and one next action—no private installation is needed.
