## 🚀 How to use

1. Give your AI assistant the **entire [runbook](linkedin-profile-optimization.md)**: attach the Markdown file, paste its full contents, or point a file-enabled agent to its path.
2. Share your current resume, target engineering roles and level, preferred locations, and remote, hybrid, or onsite preferences.
3. Log into LinkedIn yourself in the agent's authorized browser, or provide a profile export or screenshots for a recommendations-only review.
4. Ask the agent to read the whole file and use it as the guiding instructions for a read-only profile audit.
5. Review the proposed changes. If you want live updates, approve each exact batch and have the agent verify the saved fields.

> [!TIP]
> Start with the [resume runbook](../Resume/README.md) if your experience bullets need work. The approved wording gives your LinkedIn review a stronger factual starting point.

## What to expect

- A checklist covering profile copy, skills, job preferences, supporting sections, and privacy settings.
- A resume-to-profile consistency check with exact proposed wording and supporting evidence.
- Prioritized recommendations that explain any visibility or notification effects.
- Verified before-and-after values for live edits, with inaccessible items marked unavailable.

An export or screenshots are enough to review the supplied content. Inspecting account settings and applying or verifying live changes requires browser access.
