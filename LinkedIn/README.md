# LinkedIn profile

Audit your profile against your resume and target engineering roles.

## How to use

1. Share the complete [LinkedIn runbook](linkedin-profile-optimization.md), your resume, target roles and level, and your location and work-mode preferences.
2. Provide a profile export or screenshots for a content review. For a live audit, sign in yourself in the authorized browser.
3. Ask for a read-only audit. Review the proposed wording, supporting evidence and any effects on visibility or notifications.
4. For live edits, approve each exact batch. The agent reads the saved fields back and reports anything it could not verify.

See the [browser requirements](../ONBOARDING.md#tools-for-your-choices) for live access.

## Change your targets

Share the revised roles, level or locations. Reuse confirmed resume facts and review the new recommendations before approving edits.
