# Résumé intake for job discovery

Use this when setting up job discovery or reviewing changed search goals. Reuse confirmed facts and preferences. Ask for a current résumé or the relevant work history when facts are missing. Accept an attachment or a private path.

## Draft the experience summary

1. Read the résumé with the available local file tools. Check that roles and dates extracted from columns or tables still belong together. If text is unreadable or incomplete, ask for a readable copy or the missing facts. Keep the résumé and extracted notes private.
2. For each relevant role or project, record its dates, work type, and evidence for the engineering areas it supports. Use duties and outcomes to distinguish backend, frontend, full-stack, applied AI or other work. Treat an undated skill list as background. Separate professional employment, internships, academic projects, and teaching or mentoring. Mark uncertain work types for confirmation.
3. Estimate time by engineering area only where dates support it. Combine overlapping periods once within a total. When several teams or areas share one dated employer span, count it once as employer tenure. Record the areas it supports and leave dedicated time per area unknown until confirmed. Mark month-only totals as approximate, record the date range used, and keep internship time visible separately. Use the review date as the provisional end for an ongoing role and confirm it is current.

Show a short table with **Engineering area**, **Resume evidence**, and **Time or uncertainty**. Ask the user to correct the facts. Keep factual tenure separate from what job postings they want to see.

## Propose the search filters

Start from the user's current request and relevant saved preferences. Suggest role families supported by the résumé and show any adjacent family as a proposal. Keep unresolved choices open. For a rehearsal, label any temporary defaults and keep them out of the active setup.

| Filter | Confirm with the user |
| --- | --- |
| Roles and duties | Target families, adjacent roles to include, and responsibilities or technologies to exclude. |
| Job type and level | Full-time, internship, or another employment type. Which levels are required or preferred. Ask about enrollment or graduation only when relevant to the search. |
| Posting experience | Preferred posted years and the separate maximum mandatory minimum. Confirm treatment of unstated years, ranges that cross the ceiling, and any senior exception. |
| Location and work mode | Acceptable cities, onsite or hybrid requirements, remote countries or regions, and residence or relocation constraints that affect eligibility. |
| Other requirements | Any user-stated hard limits such as sponsorship, schedule, or industry. Leave unknown applicant qualifications open until confirmed. |

Read back **Hard filters**, **Preferences**, and **Open choices**. Keep answers that are already confirmed. Ask first about the unresolved choice that most changes the search, then confirm the resulting filters. Never infer current residence, work authorization, or willingness to relocate from the résumé.

For example, “Backend or applied AI, NYC, mid-level, around two years” suggests role families and a posting target. If the location choice is open, ask whether to include US remote roles open to a New York resident. If the level or ceiling is open, ask whether mid-level or two years is a limit or a priority. Apply the answer to the search without changing the person's tenure estimate.

## Hand off the confirmed choices

Save the confirmed facts and search requirements to the private records in [agent setup](agent-setup.md#1-establish-the-selected-workflow). Keep the evidence and time basis for any estimate. Use the confirmed criteria to create filters. If referral portals are selected, continue with [the company list and reusable searches](referrals.md).
