# Blind company research

Check your selected companies for open roles and save verified matches to the shared jobs tracker.

## Set up

Follow [onboarding](ONBOARDING.md) to reuse or create the shared installation. Review the [103-company starter list](blind-companies.json) or supply your own list. The starter ratings are historical and their dates are unknown. Choose companies and criteria, save the working list privately, and verify research and central saving.

You can run Blind on its own with Local Excel or Google Sheets. Review the [tools for your choices](../../ONBOARDING.md#tools-for-your-choices).

## Run

For a coordinated run, the coordinator starts Blind and saves verified findings to the tracker. To run Blind on its own, follow the [research runbook](run.md) and have the coordinator import the queued results.

Once the jobs are saved, you can request [Application Batches](<../../Application Batches/README.md>).

## Change preferences

Use [onboarding](ONBOARDING.md#change-preferences) to change companies, criteria or timing.
